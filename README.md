# Satchel: Inventory Manager

A roomy inventory window for D&D 5e characters who carry a lot: herbs, reagents, monster parts, ore, ammunition and everything else a crafter hoards.

- **Opens from any character sheet:** a Satchel button in the sheet header (and optionally on the inventory tab).
- **Automatic categories** (herbs, reagents, monster parts, metals and gems, potions, ammunition and more), plus search and your own tags.
- **Click an item to see its details** right in the list. Double-click the name to rename it, or press **E** to open the full item sheet.
- **Quick quantity changes** and **one-click merging** of duplicate stacks.
- **Bulk moves** into bags, chests and other containers.
- **Give items** to another character or to the party stash (a Group actor).
- **Weight and value totals** for everything, or just what's showing.
- **GM award window:** drag in materials from any compendium or the Items tab, tick who gets them, and hand them out in one go. Stacks grow instead of duplicating, and a short chat card records what was given.

### Settings
- Show a Satchel button on the inventory tab as well as the sheet header.
- Offer to remove a stack when its quantity reaches 0.
- Post a short chat card when items are given or awarded.

Giving items to another player's character is carried out by the GM's client, so a GM needs to be logged in for that.

Requires the D&D 5e system, version 4.0 or later (tested on 6.0.5).

## Installing

In Foundry, go to **Add-on Modules → Install Module**, paste this manifest URL and click **Install**:

```
https://github.com/charliesuits/foundry-satchel/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

Then enable it in your world under **Manage Modules**. Foundry will offer updates automatically when a new version is released.

Works with Foundry VTT v13 and v14.
